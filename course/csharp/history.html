History and Evolution of C#

C# was developed by Microsoft in the early 2000s as part of its .NET Framework initiative. The creation of C# was driven by the need for a modern, object-oriented language that would be simple to use, highly productive, and suited for the growing world of web and enterprise applications. C# continues to evolve with regular releases, adding new features to improve developer experience, performance, and maintainability. As of 2025, C# 14 and beyond may focus on further enhancements for asynchronous programming, improved diagnostics, and making the language even more expressive. Future releases of C# and features can be checked at: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/whats-new/csharp-version-history
